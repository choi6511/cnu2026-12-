Circular icon-only button. Always pass `label` — it becomes the accessible name.

```jsx
<IconButton label="닫기" variant="ghost"><i data-lucide="x"></i></IconButton>
<IconButton label="다운로드" variant="accent" size="lg"><i data-lucide="download"></i></IconButton>
```

Use Lucide at `stroke-width: 2`, 20px (sm/md) or 24px (lg).
